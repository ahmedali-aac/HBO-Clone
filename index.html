What is HBO Max?

HBO Max is a premier global streaming platform that delivers captivating stories, ranging from the highest quality in scripted programming, movies, documentaries, and true crime, to adult animation. Stream the most talked about series and blockbuster movies featuring the worlds of HBO, the DC Universe, Harry Potter, Discovery and more on HBO Max.

HBO Max is available right here on hbomax.com, via select app stores (Including the Google Play Store and the Apple App store), and through our participating partners. Availability varies by country and region. Live content may contain ads. For more info, visit our Help Center help.hbomax.com.

HBO Max is available in the United States and many international markets. Check hbomax.com for availability in your region.

4K UHD content is available on the Premium plan. You also need a compatible 4K device and a strong internet connection of at least 25 Mbps.

HBO Max works on smartphones, tablets, smart TVs, gaming consoles, and web browsers. Supported devices include Apple, Android, Roku, Amazon Fire TV, and more.
